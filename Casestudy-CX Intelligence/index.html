CASE STUDY · B2B SaaS · Enterprise CX Intelligence Platform

Reframing contact-center analytics into operational decision support

The platform turned billions of customer conversations into operational insights for enterprise contact centres. After two years in development, it had no paying customers and several stalled pilots. It could report what happened, but it couldn't help users decide what to do next.

I partnered directly with the CEO to reposition the product around a clearer customer need, using prospective-customer insight to shape direction and guide execution.

The Gap

Platform surfaced problems but left root causes unclear

AI turned millions of unstructured customer conversations into sentiment and operational insights across channels. Contact-centre leaders needed to understand why escalations increased, distinguish training gaps from staffing issues, and determine the right response.

Data revealed what had changed, but explaining why and determining next steps still required manual investigation.

The objective

Close the gap between analytical capability and business value.

Diagnosis

Three key issues surfaced.

  1. 01

    Feature-led, not goal-led

    Dashboards and reports organised the experience around features rather than business objectives, making it harder to identify which issues required attention.

  2. 02

    Data without direction

    Analytics surfaced sentiment shifts and escalation spikes but offered little guidance on their causes, business implications, or appropriate responses.

  3. 03

    Manual analysis, delayed action

    Teams still had to interpret AI-generated signals and investigate root causes manually, creating reliance on analytics expertise and slowing operational decisions.

Strategic Pivots

Navigating ambiguity to find the right product direction.

With no customers and several untested assumptions, the path forward wasn't clear. I worked with the CEO to test our thinking through product demos and conversations with prospective buyers, uncovering distinct needs across frontline agents, managers, and leadership.

These insights helped narrow the focus from presenting analytics to supporting decisions in context.

The Insight Model

From querying data to surfacing actionable insight.

The original experience relied on people knowing what to ask and how to interpret the data. We shifted the approach towards surfacing relevant intelligence by role, with explanations that connected signals to operational context.

Before

Data processed→ Manual queries→ Analyst interprets→ Delayed insight

Data expertise required · Manual investigation · Analytics bottlenecks

After

Data processed→ Relevant insights surfaced→ AI explains context→ Faster decisions

No initial query required · Contextual explanations · Insight integrated into workflows

Solution

From performance signals to targeted interventions.

Role-based dashboards highlighted each leader’s most relevant KPIs and flagged changes against visual baselines. Leaders could drill down by agent, business unit, region, or other dimensions to investigate the drivers of declining performance.

AI correlated performance signals to identify likely root causes and recommend targeted interventions, such as agent coaching or training, for manager review and approval. Actions requiring operational changes outside the platform were handed off to the relevant teams, connecting insight to action.

Impact

From stalled pilots to enterprise adoption

Takeaway

There was no playbook, so we learned our way to one.

When we started in 2023, generative AI in enterprise CX was still new territory, with no playbook for turning it into a product people would pay for. I came into this work focused on what the technology could do. What moved us forward was understanding the as-is process, listening to prospective customers, letting go of assumptions that didn't hold, and following where their needs led. It's a lesson I keep coming back to: stay curious, stay close to the problem, adapt as capabilities evolve, and let the opportunity reveal itself.
